## Questions

**How has rent moved in Iowa?** The median two-bedroom rent of Iowa's 99 counties rose from $363 in FY1995 to $927 in FY2027, 155.4%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Iowa is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Harrison County](./Iowa/Harrison%20County.md), [Mills County](./Iowa/Mills%20County.md) and [Pottawattamie County](./Iowa/Pottawattamie%20County.md), at $1,450 a month, and cheapest in [Allamakee County](./Iowa/Allamakee%20County.md), [Appanoose County](./Iowa/Appanoose%20County.md), [Audubon County](./Iowa/Audubon%20County.md) and 47 more, at $927. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Iowa does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 23.4% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Iowa's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Harrison County](./Iowa/Harrison%20County.md), [Mills County](./Iowa/Mills%20County.md) and [Pottawattamie County](./Iowa/Pottawattamie%20County.md) (28.8%), least in [Benton County](./Iowa/Benton%20County.md) (20.3%).

**Has rent outrun income in Iowa?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 66 of Iowa's 99 counties with both, and slower in 33.
