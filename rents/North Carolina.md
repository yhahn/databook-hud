## Questions

**How has rent moved in North Carolina?** The median two-bedroom rent of North Carolina's 100 counties rose from $390 in FY1995 to $1,145 in FY2027, 194%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in North Carolina is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Buncombe County](./North%20Carolina/Buncombe%20County.md), [Henderson County](./North%20Carolina/Henderson%20County.md) and [Madison County](./North%20Carolina/Madison%20County.md), at $1,866 a month, and cheapest in [Ashe County](./North%20Carolina/Ashe%20County.md), [Avery County](./North%20Carolina/Avery%20County.md), [Beaufort County](./North%20Carolina/Beaufort%20County.md) and 32 more, at $1,012. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in North Carolina does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 30% of the very low income limit for four in the median of North Carolina's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Gates County](./North%20Carolina/Gates%20County.md) (46.3%), least in [Lincoln County](./North%20Carolina/Lincoln%20County.md) (24%).

**Has rent outrun income in North Carolina?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 85 of North Carolina's 100 counties with both, and slower in 15.
