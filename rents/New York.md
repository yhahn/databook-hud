## Questions

**How has rent moved in New York?** The median two-bedroom rent of New York's 62 counties rose from $536 in FY1995 to $1,385 in FY2027, 158.4%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in New York is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Bronx County](./New%20York/Bronx%20County.md), [Kings County](./New%20York/Kings%20County.md), [New York County](./New%20York/New%20York%20County.md) and 5 more, at $2,971 a month, and cheapest in [Allegany County](./New%20York/Allegany%20County.md), [Cattaraugus County](./New%20York/Cattaraugus%20County.md), [Chautauqua County](./New%20York/Chautauqua%20County.md) and 4 more, at $1,014. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in New York does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 32.1% of the very low income limit for four in the median of New York's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Bronx County](./New%20York/Bronx%20County.md), [Kings County](./New%20York/Kings%20County.md), [New York County](./New%20York/New%20York%20County.md) and 5 more (41.2%), least in [Wyoming County](./New%20York/Wyoming%20County.md) (25.4%).

**Has rent outrun income in New York?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 61 of New York's 62 counties with both, and slower in 1.
