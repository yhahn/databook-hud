## Questions

**How has rent moved in Montana?** The median two-bedroom rent of Montana's 56 counties rose from $370 in FY1995 to $1,310 in FY2027, 254.1%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Montana is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Gallatin County](./Montana/Gallatin%20County.md), at $2,109 a month, and cheapest in [Phillips County](./Montana/Phillips%20County.md), at $1,014. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Montana does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 32.9% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Montana's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Broadwater County](./Montana/Broadwater%20County.md) (41.2%), least in [Phillips County](./Montana/Phillips%20County.md) (25.7%).

**Has rent outrun income in Montana?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 50 of Montana's 56 counties with both, and slower in 6.
