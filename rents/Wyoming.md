## Questions

**How has rent moved in Wyoming?** The median two-bedroom rent of Wyoming's 23 counties rose from $356 in FY1995 to $1,016 in FY2027, 185.4%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Wyoming is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Teton County](./Wyoming/Teton%20County.md), at $1,683 a month, and cheapest in [Albany County](./Wyoming/Albany%20County.md), [Big Horn County](./Wyoming/Big%20Horn%20County.md), [Crook County](./Wyoming/Crook%20County.md) and 8 more, at $999. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Wyoming does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 23.3% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Wyoming's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Niobrara County](./Wyoming/Niobrara%20County.md) (28.6%), least in [Lincoln County](./Wyoming/Lincoln%20County.md) (20.3%).

**Has rent outrun income in Wyoming?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 19 of Wyoming's 23 counties with both, and slower in 4.
