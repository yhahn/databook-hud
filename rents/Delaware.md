## Questions

**How has rent moved in Delaware?** The median two-bedroom rent of Delaware's 3 counties rose from $509 in FY1995 to $1,629 in FY2027, 220%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Delaware is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [New Castle County](./Delaware/New%20Castle%20County.md), at $1,860 a month, and cheapest in [Sussex County](./Delaware/Sussex%20County.md), at $1,498. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Delaware does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 32.7% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Delaware's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [New Castle County](./Delaware/New%20Castle%20County.md) (35.4%), least in [Sussex County](./Delaware/Sussex%20County.md) (32.5%).

**Has rent outrun income in Delaware?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 3 of Delaware's 3 counties with both, and slower in 0.
