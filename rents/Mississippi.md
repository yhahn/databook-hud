## Questions

**How has rent moved in Mississippi?** The median two-bedroom rent of Mississippi's 82 counties rose from $300 in FY1995 to $905 in FY2027, 201.7%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Mississippi is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Forrest County](./Mississippi/Forrest%20County.md), [Lamar County](./Mississippi/Lamar%20County.md) and [Perry County](./Mississippi/Perry%20County.md), at $1,316 a month, and cheapest in [Adams County](./Mississippi/Adams%20County.md), [Alcorn County](./Mississippi/Alcorn%20County.md), [Amite County](./Mississippi/Amite%20County.md) and 45 more, at $905. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Mississippi does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 28.1% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Mississippi's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Tunica County](./Mississippi/Tunica%20County.md) (39.5%), least in [Carroll County](./Mississippi/Carroll%20County.md) (24.8%).

**Has rent outrun income in Mississippi?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 57 of Mississippi's 82 counties with both, and slower in 25.
