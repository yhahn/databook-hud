Its income limits are on its [limits page](../limits/Maryland.md), its tax credit housing on its [tax credits page](../lihtc/Maryland.md).

## Questions

**How has rent moved in Maryland?** The median two-bedroom rent of Maryland's 24 counties rose from $592 in FY1995 to $1,875 in FY2027, 216.7%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Maryland is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Charles County](./Maryland/Charles%20County.md), [Frederick County](./Maryland/Frederick%20County.md), [Montgomery County](./Maryland/Montgomery%20County.md) and [Prince George's County](./Maryland/Prince%20George%27s%20County.md), at $2,438 a month, and cheapest in [Allegany County](./Maryland/Allegany%20County.md) and [Garrett County](./Maryland/Garrett%20County.md), at $1,014. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Maryland does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 32.5% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Maryland's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Cecil County](./Maryland/Cecil%20County.md) (35.4%), least in [Allegany County](./Maryland/Allegany%20County.md) and [Garrett County](./Maryland/Garrett%20County.md) (24.6%).

**Has rent outrun income in Maryland?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 17 of Maryland's 24 counties with both, and slower in 7.
