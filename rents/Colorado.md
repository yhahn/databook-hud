## Questions

**How has rent moved in Colorado?** The median two-bedroom rent of Colorado's 64 counties rose from $412 in FY1995 to $1,375 in FY2027, 233.6%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Colorado is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Summit County](./Colorado/Summit%20County.md), at $2,766 a month, and cheapest in [Baca County](./Colorado/Baca%20County.md), [Conejos County](./Colorado/Conejos%20County.md), [Costilla County](./Colorado/Costilla%20County.md) and [Sedgwick County](./Colorado/Sedgwick%20County.md), at $1,014. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Colorado does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 28.9% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Colorado's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Eagle County](./Colorado/Eagle%20County.md) (41.2%), least in [Baca County](./Colorado/Baca%20County.md), [Conejos County](./Colorado/Conejos%20County.md), [Costilla County](./Colorado/Costilla%20County.md), [Otero County](./Colorado/Otero%20County.md), [Prowers County](./Colorado/Prowers%20County.md) and [Sedgwick County](./Colorado/Sedgwick%20County.md) (24%).

**Has rent outrun income in Colorado?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 48 of Colorado's 64 counties with both, and slower in 16.
