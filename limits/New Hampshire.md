# Income Limits: New Hampshire

HUD's Section 8 income limits for New Hampshire, fiscal years 1998 to 2026: 259 towns, as HUD lists them in FY2026. Before FY2006 HUD keyed New England's limits by county, not town: those rows are kept on each county's page, under Counties before 2006, and each town names its own as `County before 2006`. Each county's page lists its towns. How rent and income compare across its places, and how they have moved, is on its [rents page](./../rents/New%20Hampshire.md).
