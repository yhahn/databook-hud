## Questions

**How has rent moved in Indiana?** The median two-bedroom rent of Indiana's 92 counties rose from $391 in FY1995 to $1,086 in FY2027, 177.6%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Indiana is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Boone County](./Indiana/Boone%20County.md), [Brown County](./Indiana/Brown%20County.md), [Hamilton County](./Indiana/Hamilton%20County.md) and 6 more, at $1,536 a month, and cheapest in [Adams County](./Indiana/Adams%20County.md), [Blackford County](./Indiana/Blackford%20County.md), [Cass County](./Indiana/Cass%20County.md) and 27 more, at $1,014. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Indiana does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 27.3% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Indiana's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [St. Joseph County](./Indiana/St.%20Joseph%20County.md) (35.2%), least in [Warren County](./Indiana/Warren%20County.md) (21.6%).

**Has rent outrun income in Indiana?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 84 of Indiana's 92 counties with both, and slower in 8.
