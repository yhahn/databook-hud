## Questions

**How has rent moved in New Hampshire?** The median two-bedroom rent of New Hampshire's 259 towns rose from $615 in FY1995 to $1,734 in FY2027, 182%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in New Hampshire is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Seabrook town](./New%20Hampshire/Rockingham%20County/Seabrook%20town.md) and [South Hampton town](./New%20Hampshire/Rockingham%20County/South%20Hampton%20town.md), at $3,008 a month, and cheapest in [Atkinson and Gilmanton Academy grant](./New%20Hampshire/Coos%20County/Atkinson%20and%20Gilmanton%20Academy%20grant.md), [Beans grant](./New%20Hampshire/Coos%20County/Beans%20grant.md), [Beans purchase](./New%20Hampshire/Coos%20County/Beans%20purchase.md) and 40 more, at $1,195. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in New Hampshire does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 36.8% of the very low income limit for four in the median of New Hampshire's towns (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Seabrook town](./New%20Hampshire/Rockingham%20County/Seabrook%20town.md) and [South Hampton town](./New%20Hampshire/Rockingham%20County/South%20Hampton%20town.md) (41.2%), least in [Atkinson and Gilmanton Academy grant](./New%20Hampshire/Coos%20County/Atkinson%20and%20Gilmanton%20Academy%20grant.md), [Beans grant](./New%20Hampshire/Coos%20County/Beans%20grant.md), [Beans purchase](./New%20Hampshire/Coos%20County/Beans%20purchase.md) and 40 more (26.3%).

**Has rent outrun income in New Hampshire?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 259 of New Hampshire's 259 towns with both, and slower in 0.
