## Questions

**How has rent moved in Virginia?** The median two-bedroom rent of Virginia's 133 counties rose from $433 in FY1995 to $1,262 in FY2027, 191.5%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Virginia is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Arlington County](./Virginia/Arlington%20County.md), [Clarke County](./Virginia/Clarke%20County.md), [Fairfax County](./Virginia/Fairfax%20County.md) and 11 more, at $2,438 a month, and cheapest in [Alleghany County](./Virginia/Alleghany%20County.md), [Bland County](./Virginia/Bland%20County.md), [Brunswick County](./Virginia/Brunswick%20County.md) and 23 more, at $943. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Virginia does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 30.7% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Virginia's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Gloucester County](./Virginia/Gloucester%20County.md), [Isle of Wight County](./Virginia/Isle%20of%20Wight%20County.md), [James City County](./Virginia/James%20City%20County.md) and 11 more (38.2%), least in [Surry County](./Virginia/Surry%20County.md) (24.9%).

**Has rent outrun income in Virginia?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 112 of Virginia's 133 counties with both, and slower in 21.
