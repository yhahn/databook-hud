## Questions

**How has rent moved in Guam?** Guam's two-bedroom rent rose from $907 in FY1995 to $1,449 in FY2027, 59.8%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Guam is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** Guam is one place, at $1,449 for two bedrooms in FY2027.

**Where in Guam does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of Guam's FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 41.2% of its very low income limit for four (28.8% in the nation's median county).

**Has rent outrun income in Guam?** From FY2006 to FY2026 Guam's two-bedroom rent rose 76.9% and its median family income 69.6%.
