## Questions

**How has rent moved in the Northern Mariana Islands?** The Northern Mariana Islands's two-bedroom rent rose from $642 in FY2016 to $988 in FY2027, 53.9%; the nation's median county's rose 57.8% in the same years.

**Where in the Northern Mariana Islands is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** The Northern Mariana Islands is one place, at $988 for two bedrooms in FY2027.

**Where in the Northern Mariana Islands does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the Northern Mariana Islands's FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 41.2% of its very low income limit for four (28.8% in the nation's median county).

**Has rent outrun income in the Northern Mariana Islands?** The book does not say for the Northern Mariana Islands: HUD's files give it no value this needs.
