## Questions

**How has rent moved in Oklahoma?** The median two-bedroom rent of Oklahoma's 77 counties rose from $311 in FY1995 to $916 in FY2027, 194.5%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Oklahoma is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Canadian County](./Oklahoma/Canadian%20County.md), [Cleveland County](./Oklahoma/Cleveland%20County.md), [Logan County](./Oklahoma/Logan%20County.md), [McClain County](./Oklahoma/McClain%20County.md) and [Oklahoma County](./Oklahoma/Oklahoma%20County.md), at $1,264 a month, and cheapest in [Adair County](./Oklahoma/Adair%20County.md), [Alfalfa County](./Oklahoma/Alfalfa%20County.md), [Atoka County](./Oklahoma/Atoka%20County.md) and 38 more, at $916. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Oklahoma does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 29.6% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Oklahoma's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Beckham County](./Oklahoma/Beckham%20County.md) (33%), least in [Noble County](./Oklahoma/Noble%20County.md) (23.6%).

**Has rent outrun income in Oklahoma?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 72 of Oklahoma's 77 counties with both, and slower in 5.
