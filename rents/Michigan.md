## Questions

**How has rent moved in Michigan?** The median two-bedroom rent of Michigan's 83 counties rose from $392 in FY1995 to $1,085 in FY2027, 176.8%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Michigan is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Washtenaw County](./Michigan/Washtenaw%20County.md), at $1,804 a month, and cheapest in [Alcona County](./Michigan/Alcona%20County.md), [Alpena County](./Michigan/Alpena%20County.md), [Arenac County](./Michigan/Arenac%20County.md) and 21 more, at $1,014. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Michigan does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 28.5% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Michigan's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Muskegon County](./Michigan/Muskegon%20County.md) (35.5%), least in [Charlevoix County](./Michigan/Charlevoix%20County.md) (25.6%).

**Has rent outrun income in Michigan?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 78 of Michigan's 83 counties with both, and slower in 5.
