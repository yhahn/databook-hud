## Questions

**How has rent moved in Missouri?** The median two-bedroom rent of Missouri's 116 counties rose from $315 in FY1995 to $962 in FY2027, 205.9%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Missouri is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Caldwell County](./Missouri/Caldwell%20County.md), [Cass County](./Missouri/Cass%20County.md), [Clay County](./Missouri/Clay%20County.md) and 5 more, at $1,546 a month, and cheapest in [Adair County](./Missouri/Adair%20County.md), [Atchison County](./Missouri/Atchison%20County.md), [Audrain County](./Missouri/Audrain%20County.md) and 56 more, at $962. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Missouri does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 27.5% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Missouri's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Taney County](./Missouri/Taney%20County.md) (31.9%), least in [Cole County](./Missouri/Cole%20County.md) and [Osage County](./Missouri/Osage%20County.md) (20.7%).

**Has rent outrun income in Missouri?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 100 of Missouri's 116 counties with both, and slower in 16.
