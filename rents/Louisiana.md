## Questions

**How has rent moved in Louisiana?** The median two-bedroom rent of Louisiana's 64 counties rose from $326 in FY1995 to $995 in FY2027, 205.7%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Louisiana is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Jefferson Parish](./Louisiana/Jefferson%20Parish.md), [Orleans Parish](./Louisiana/Orleans%20Parish.md), [Plaquemines Parish](./Louisiana/Plaquemines%20Parish.md), [St. Bernard Parish](./Louisiana/St.%20Bernard%20Parish.md), [St. Charles Parish](./Louisiana/St.%20Charles%20Parish.md) and [St. John the Baptist Parish](./Louisiana/St.%20John%20the%20Baptist%20Parish.md), at $1,480 a month, and cheapest in [Bienville Parish](./Louisiana/Bienville%20Parish.md), [Caldwell Parish](./Louisiana/Caldwell%20Parish.md), [Catahoula Parish](./Louisiana/Catahoula%20Parish.md) and 17 more, at $892. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Louisiana does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 30.5% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Louisiana's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Jefferson Parish](./Louisiana/Jefferson%20Parish.md), [Orleans Parish](./Louisiana/Orleans%20Parish.md), [Plaquemines Parish](./Louisiana/Plaquemines%20Parish.md), [St. Bernard Parish](./Louisiana/St.%20Bernard%20Parish.md), [St. Charles Parish](./Louisiana/St.%20Charles%20Parish.md) and [St. John the Baptist Parish](./Louisiana/St.%20John%20the%20Baptist%20Parish.md) (36.1%), least in [St. James Parish](./Louisiana/St.%20James%20Parish.md) (23.5%).

**Has rent outrun income in Louisiana?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 45 of Louisiana's 64 counties with both, and slower in 19.
