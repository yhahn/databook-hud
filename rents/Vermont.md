## Questions

**How has rent moved in Vermont?** The median two-bedroom rent of Vermont's 256 towns rose from $540 in FY1995 to $1,558 in FY2027, 188.5%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Vermont is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Bolton town](./Vermont/Chittenden%20County/Bolton%20town.md), [Buels gore](./Vermont/Chittenden%20County/Buels%20gore.md), [Burlington city](./Vermont/Chittenden%20County/Burlington%20city.md) and 36 more, at $2,165 a month, and cheapest in [Averill town](./Vermont/Essex%20County/Averill%20town.md), [Avery's gore](./Vermont/Essex%20County/Avery%27s%20gore.md), [Bloomfield town](./Vermont/Essex%20County/Bloomfield%20town.md) and 16 more, at $1,167. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Vermont does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 30.8% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Vermont's towns (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Bolton town](./Vermont/Chittenden%20County/Bolton%20town.md), [Buels gore](./Vermont/Chittenden%20County/Buels%20gore.md), [Burlington city](./Vermont/Chittenden%20County/Burlington%20city.md) and 36 more (41.2%), least in [Averill town](./Vermont/Essex%20County/Averill%20town.md), [Avery's gore](./Vermont/Essex%20County/Avery%27s%20gore.md), [Bloomfield town](./Vermont/Essex%20County/Bloomfield%20town.md) and 16 more (23.1%).

**Has rent outrun income in Vermont?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 234 of Vermont's 253 towns with both, and slower in 19.
