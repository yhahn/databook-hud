## Questions

**How has rent moved in Arkansas?** The median two-bedroom rent of Arkansas's 75 counties rose from $325 in FY1995 to $909 in FY2027, 179.7%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Arkansas is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Crittenden County](./Arkansas/Crittenden%20County.md), at $1,301 a month, and cheapest in [Ashley County](./Arkansas/Ashley%20County.md), [Boone County](./Arkansas/Boone%20County.md), [Bradley County](./Arkansas/Bradley%20County.md) and 40 more, at $909. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Arkansas does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 29.7% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Arkansas's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Craighead County](./Arkansas/Craighead%20County.md) (33%), least in [Prairie County](./Arkansas/Prairie%20County.md) (25%).

**Has rent outrun income in Arkansas?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 57 of Arkansas's 75 counties with both, and slower in 18.
