## Questions

**How has rent moved in Massachusetts?** The median two-bedroom rent of Massachusetts's 351 towns rose from $683 in FY1995 to $2,367 in FY2027, 246.6%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Massachusetts is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Nantucket town](./Massachusetts/Nantucket%20County/Nantucket%20town.md), at $3,583 a month, and cheapest in [Athol town](./Massachusetts/Worcester%20County/Athol%20town.md), [Hardwick town](./Massachusetts/Worcester%20County/Hardwick%20town.md), [Hubbardston town](./Massachusetts/Worcester%20County/Hubbardston%20town.md) and 5 more, at $1,659. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Massachusetts does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 37.5% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Massachusetts's towns (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Nantucket town](./Massachusetts/Nantucket%20County/Nantucket%20town.md) (41.4%), least in [Acushnet town](./Massachusetts/Bristol%20County/Acushnet%20town.md), [Dartmouth town](./Massachusetts/Bristol%20County/Dartmouth%20town.md), [Fairhaven town](./Massachusetts/Bristol%20County/Fairhaven%20town.md), [Freetown town](./Massachusetts/Bristol%20County/Freetown%20town.md) and [New Bedford city](./Massachusetts/Bristol%20County/New%20Bedford%20city.md) (27.9%).

**Has rent outrun income in Massachusetts?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 344 of Massachusetts's 351 towns with both, and slower in 7.
