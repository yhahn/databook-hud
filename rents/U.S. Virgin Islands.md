## Questions

**How has rent moved in the U.S. Virgin Islands?** The median two-bedroom rent of the U.S. Virgin Islands's 3 counties rose from $682 in FY1995 to $1,509 in FY2027, 121.3%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in the U.S. Virgin Islands is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [St. John](./U.S.%20Virgin%20Islands/St.%20John.md), at $2,025 a month, and cheapest in [St. Croix](./U.S.%20Virgin%20Islands/St.%20Croix.md), at $1,237. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in the U.S. Virgin Islands does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 39.5% of the very low income limit for four in the median of the U.S. Virgin Islands's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [St. Thomas](./U.S.%20Virgin%20Islands/St.%20Thomas.md) (39.9%), least in [St. Croix](./U.S.%20Virgin%20Islands/St.%20Croix.md) (39%).

**Has rent outrun income in the U.S. Virgin Islands?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 1 of the U.S. Virgin Islands's 3 counties with both, and slower in 2.
