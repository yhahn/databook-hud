## Questions

**How has rent moved in American Samoa?** American Samoa's two-bedroom rent rose from $653 in FY2016 to $942 in FY2027, 44.3%; the nation's median county's rose 57.8% in the same years.

**Where in American Samoa is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** American Samoa is one place, at $942 for two bedrooms in FY2027.

**Where in American Samoa does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of American Samoa's FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 39.3% of its very low income limit for four (28.8% in the nation's median county).

**Has rent outrun income in American Samoa?** The book does not say for American Samoa: HUD's files give it no value this needs.
