## Questions

**How has rent moved in Ohio?** The median two-bedroom rent of Ohio's 88 counties rose from $402 in FY1995 to $1,048 in FY2027, 160.7%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Ohio is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Union County](./Ohio/Union%20County.md), at $1,607 a month, and cheapest in [Adams County](./Ohio/Adams%20County.md), [Ashland County](./Ohio/Ashland%20County.md), [Ashtabula County](./Ohio/Ashtabula%20County.md) and 27 more, at $1,003. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Ohio does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 26.6% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Ohio's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Portage County](./Ohio/Portage%20County.md) and [Summit County](./Ohio/Summit%20County.md) (31.3%), least in [Auglaize County](./Ohio/Auglaize%20County.md) (22.7%).

**Has rent outrun income in Ohio?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 84 of Ohio's 88 counties with both, and slower in 4.
