## Questions

**How has rent moved in Minnesota?** The median two-bedroom rent of Minnesota's 87 counties rose from $369 in FY1995 to $1,048 in FY2027, 184%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Minnesota is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Anoka County](./Minnesota/Anoka%20County.md), [Carver County](./Minnesota/Carver%20County.md), [Chisago County](./Minnesota/Chisago%20County.md) and 8 more, at $1,734 a month, and cheapest in [Big Stone County](./Minnesota/Big%20Stone%20County.md), [Cass County](./Minnesota/Cass%20County.md), [Chippewa County](./Minnesota/Chippewa%20County.md) and 32 more, at $1,014. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Minnesota does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 23.8% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Minnesota's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Anoka County](./Minnesota/Anoka%20County.md), [Carver County](./Minnesota/Carver%20County.md), [Chisago County](./Minnesota/Chisago%20County.md) and 8 more (31.2%), least in [Kittson County](./Minnesota/Kittson%20County.md) (21.8%).

**Has rent outrun income in Minnesota?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 72 of Minnesota's 87 counties with both, and slower in 15.
