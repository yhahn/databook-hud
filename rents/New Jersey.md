## Questions

**How has rent moved in New Jersey?** The median two-bedroom rent of New Jersey's 21 counties rose from $828 in FY1995 to $2,233 in FY2027, 169.7%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in New Jersey is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Hudson County](./New%20Jersey/Hudson%20County.md), at $2,792 a month, and cheapest in [Cumberland County](./New%20Jersey/Cumberland%20County.md), at $1,704. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in New Jersey does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 38.2% of the very low income limit for four in the median of New Jersey's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Hudson County](./New%20Jersey/Hudson%20County.md) (45%), least in [Mercer County](./New%20Jersey/Mercer%20County.md) (33.5%).

**Has rent outrun income in New Jersey?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 21 of New Jersey's 21 counties with both, and slower in 0.
