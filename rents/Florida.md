## Questions

**How has rent moved in Florida?** The median two-bedroom rent of Florida's 67 counties rose from $439 in FY1995 to $1,474 in FY2027, 235.8%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Florida is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Miami-Dade County](./Florida/Miami-Dade%20County.md), at $2,564 a month, and cheapest in [Calhoun County](./Florida/Calhoun%20County.md), [Hamilton County](./Florida/Hamilton%20County.md) and [Taylor County](./Florida/Taylor%20County.md), at $953. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Florida does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 37.9% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Florida's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Gulf County](./Florida/Gulf%20County.md) (47.4%), least in [Baker County](./Florida/Baker%20County.md) (26.3%).

**Has rent outrun income in Florida?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 67 of Florida's 67 counties with both, and slower in 0.
