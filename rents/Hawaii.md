HUD's Fair Market Rents for Hawaii, fiscal years 1983 to 2027: 5 counties, each with its rents by bedroom count and the FMR areas it has sat in. 3 of them have changed FMR area at least once (not counting 2006, when HUD renumbered every area). Its income limits are on its [limits page](../limits/Hawaii.md), its tax credit housing on its [tax credits page](../lihtc/Hawaii.md).

## Questions

**How has rent moved in Hawaii?** The median two-bedroom rent of Hawaii's 5 counties rose from $1,051 in FY1995 to $2,326 in FY2027, 121.3%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Hawaii is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Maui County](./Hawaii/Maui%20County.md), at $2,656 a month, and cheapest in [Hawaii County](./Hawaii/Hawaii%20County.md), at $2,081. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Hawaii does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 41.2% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Hawaii's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Kalawao County](./Hawaii/Kalawao%20County.md) (49.1%), least in [Honolulu County](./Hawaii/Honolulu%20County.md) (41.2%).

**Has rent outrun income in Hawaii?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 4 of Hawaii's 5 counties with both, and slower in 1.
