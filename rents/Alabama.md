## Questions

**How has rent moved in Alabama?** The median two-bedroom rent of Alabama's 67 counties rose from $303 in FY1995 to $975 in FY2027, 221.8%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Alabama is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Baldwin County](./Alabama/Baldwin%20County.md), at $1,434 a month, and cheapest in [Barbour County](./Alabama/Barbour%20County.md), [Bullock County](./Alabama/Bullock%20County.md), [Choctaw County](./Alabama/Choctaw%20County.md) and 23 more, at $921. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Alabama does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 26.7% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Alabama's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Greene County](./Alabama/Greene%20County.md) (36.9%), least in [Marengo County](./Alabama/Marengo%20County.md) (22.4%).

**Has rent outrun income in Alabama?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 59 of Alabama's 67 counties with both, and slower in 8.
