## Questions

**How has rent moved in the District of Columbia?** The District of Columbia's two-bedroom rent rose from $834 in FY1995 to $2,438 in FY2027, 192.3%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in the District of Columbia is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** The District of Columbia is one place, at $2,438 for two bedrooms in FY2027.

**Where in the District of Columbia does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the District of Columbia's FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 32.5% of its very low income limit for four (28.8% in the nation's median county).

**Has rent outrun income in the District of Columbia?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the District of Columbia's two-bedroom rent rose 83.3% and its median family income 83.9%.
