HUD's Fair Market Rents for Arizona, fiscal years 1983 to 2027: 15 counties, each with its rents by bedroom count and the FMR areas it has sat in. 6 of them have changed FMR area at least once (not counting 2006, when HUD renumbered every area). Its income limits are on its [limits page](../limits/Arizona.md), its tax credit housing on its [tax credits page](../lihtc/Arizona.md).

## Questions

**How has rent moved in Arizona?** The median two-bedroom rent of Arizona's 15 counties rose from $462 in FY1995 to $1,244 in FY2027, 169.3%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Arizona is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Maricopa County](./Arizona/Maricopa%20County.md) and [Pinal County](./Arizona/Pinal%20County.md), at $1,734 a month, and cheapest in [La Paz County](./Arizona/La%20Paz%20County.md), at $1,038. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Arizona does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 41% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Arizona's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Gila County](./Arizona/Gila%20County.md) (42.3%), least in [Greenlee County](./Arizona/Greenlee%20County.md) (33.1%).

**Has rent outrun income in Arizona?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 14 of Arizona's 15 counties with both, and slower in 1.
