## Questions

**How has rent moved in California?** The median two-bedroom rent of California's 58 counties rose from $578 in FY1995 to $1,725 in FY2027, 198.4%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in California is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Santa Cruz County](./California/Santa%20Cruz%20County.md), at $4,254 a month, and cheapest in [Modoc County](./California/Modoc%20County.md), at $1,084. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in California does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 38.8% of the very low income limit for four in the median of California's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [San Benito County](./California/San%20Benito%20County.md) (47.4%), least in [Modoc County](./California/Modoc%20County.md) (27.4%).

**Has rent outrun income in California?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 56 of California's 58 counties with both, and slower in 2.
