## Questions

**How has rent moved in Oregon?** The median two-bedroom rent of Oregon's 36 counties rose from $440 in FY1995 to $1,325 in FY2027, 201%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Oregon is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Deschutes County](./Oregon/Deschutes%20County.md), at $1,893 a month, and cheapest in [Baker County](./Oregon/Baker%20County.md), [Gilliam County](./Oregon/Gilliam%20County.md), [Harney County](./Oregon/Harney%20County.md), [Malheur County](./Oregon/Malheur%20County.md), [Morrow County](./Oregon/Morrow%20County.md) and [Wheeler County](./Oregon/Wheeler%20County.md), at $1,014. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Oregon does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 36% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Oregon's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Linn County](./Oregon/Linn%20County.md) (41.2%), least in [Morrow County](./Oregon/Morrow%20County.md) (27.4%).

**Has rent outrun income in Oregon?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 34 of Oregon's 36 counties with both, and slower in 2.
