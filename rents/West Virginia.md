## Questions

**How has rent moved in West Virginia?** The median two-bedroom rent of West Virginia's 55 counties rose from $327 in FY1995 to $955 in FY2027, 192%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in West Virginia is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Hampshire County](./West%20Virginia/Hampshire%20County.md), at $1,505 a month, and cheapest in [Barbour County](./West%20Virginia/Barbour%20County.md), [Boone County](./West%20Virginia/Boone%20County.md), [Braxton County](./West%20Virginia/Braxton%20County.md) and 23 more, at $944. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in West Virginia does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 27.8% of the very low income limit for four in the median of West Virginia's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Hampshire County](./West%20Virginia/Hampshire%20County.md) (35.1%), least in [Mineral County](./West%20Virginia/Mineral%20County.md) (22%).

**Has rent outrun income in West Virginia?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 45 of West Virginia's 55 counties with both, and slower in 10.
