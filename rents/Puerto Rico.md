## Questions

**How has rent moved in Puerto Rico?** The median two-bedroom rent of Puerto Rico's 78 counties rose from $446 in FY1995 to $613 in FY2027, 37.4%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Puerto Rico is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Aguas Buenas Municipio](./Puerto%20Rico/Aguas%20Buenas%20Municipio.md), [Barceloneta Municipio](./Puerto%20Rico/Barceloneta%20Municipio.md), [Bayamón Municipio](./Puerto%20Rico/Bayam%C3%B3n%20Municipio.md) and 25 more, at $706 a month, and cheapest in [Adjuntas Municipio](./Puerto%20Rico/Adjuntas%20Municipio.md), [Arroyo Municipio](./Puerto%20Rico/Arroyo%20Municipio.md), [Coamo Municipio](./Puerto%20Rico/Coamo%20Municipio.md) and 12 more, at $517. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Puerto Rico does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 38.4% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Puerto Rico's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Adjuntas Municipio](./Puerto%20Rico/Adjuntas%20Municipio.md) (41.2%), least in [Caguas Municipio](./Puerto%20Rico/Caguas%20Municipio.md), [Cayey Municipio](./Puerto%20Rico/Cayey%20Municipio.md), [Cidra Municipio](./Puerto%20Rico/Cidra%20Municipio.md), [Gurabo Municipio](./Puerto%20Rico/Gurabo%20Municipio.md) and [San Lorenzo Municipio](./Puerto%20Rico/San%20Lorenzo%20Municipio.md) (35.5%).

**Has rent outrun income in Puerto Rico?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 0 of Puerto Rico's 78 counties with both, and slower in 78.
