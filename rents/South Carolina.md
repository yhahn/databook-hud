## Questions

**How has rent moved in South Carolina?** The median two-bedroom rent of South Carolina's 46 counties rose from $365 in FY1995 to $1,098 in FY2027, 200.7%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in South Carolina is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Beaufort County](./South%20Carolina/Beaufort%20County.md), at $2,010 a month, and cheapest in [Abbeville County](./South%20Carolina/Abbeville%20County.md), [Allendale County](./South%20Carolina/Allendale%20County.md), [Cherokee County](./South%20Carolina/Cherokee%20County.md) and 10 more, at $885. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in South Carolina does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 30.2% of the very low income limit for four in the median of South Carolina's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Jasper County](./South%20Carolina/Jasper%20County.md) (44.3%), least in [Lancaster County](./South%20Carolina/Lancaster%20County.md) (23.7%).

**Has rent outrun income in South Carolina?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 44 of South Carolina's 46 counties with both, and slower in 2.
