HUD's Fair Market Rents for Idaho, fiscal years 1983 to 2027: 44 counties, each with its rents by bedroom count and the FMR areas it has sat in. 6 of them have changed FMR area at least once (not counting 2006, when HUD renumbered every area). Its income limits are on its [limits page](../limits/Idaho.md), its tax credit housing on its [tax credits page](../lihtc/Idaho.md).

## Questions

**How has rent moved in Idaho?** The median two-bedroom rent of Idaho's 44 counties rose from $317 in FY1995 to $1,127 in FY2027, 255.9%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Idaho is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Blaine County](./Idaho/Blaine%20County.md), at $1,673 a month, and cheapest in [Adams County](./Idaho/Adams%20County.md), [Caribou County](./Idaho/Caribou%20County.md), [Clark County](./Idaho/Clark%20County.md), [Fremont County](./Idaho/Fremont%20County.md), [Lewis County](./Idaho/Lewis%20County.md) and [Power County](./Idaho/Power%20County.md), at $1,014. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Idaho does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 28.9% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Idaho's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Ada County](./Idaho/Ada%20County.md), [Boise County](./Idaho/Boise%20County.md), [Canyon County](./Idaho/Canyon%20County.md) and [Owyhee County](./Idaho/Owyhee%20County.md) (35.9%), least in [Bingham County](./Idaho/Bingham%20County.md) (24.6%).

**Has rent outrun income in Idaho?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 29 of Idaho's 44 counties with both, and slower in 15.
