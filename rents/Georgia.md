## Questions

**How has rent moved in Georgia?** The median two-bedroom rent of Georgia's 159 counties rose from $352 in FY1995 to $1,058 in FY2027, 200.6%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Georgia is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Barrow County](./Georgia/Barrow%20County.md), [Bartow County](./Georgia/Bartow%20County.md), [Carroll County](./Georgia/Carroll%20County.md) and 21 more, at $1,762 a month, and cheapest in [Appling County](./Georgia/Appling%20County.md), [Atkinson County](./Georgia/Atkinson%20County.md), [Bacon County](./Georgia/Bacon%20County.md) and 63 more, at $1,002. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Georgia does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 31.2% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Georgia's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Liberty County](./Georgia/Liberty%20County.md) (41.2%), least in [Greene County](./Georgia/Greene%20County.md) (22.8%).

**Has rent outrun income in Georgia?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 154 of Georgia's 159 counties with both, and slower in 5.
