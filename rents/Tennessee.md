## Questions

**How has rent moved in Tennessee?** The median two-bedroom rent of Tennessee's 95 counties rose from $325 in FY1995 to $1,048 in FY2027, 222.5%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Tennessee is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Cannon County](./Tennessee/Cannon%20County.md), [Cheatham County](./Tennessee/Cheatham%20County.md), [Davidson County](./Tennessee/Davidson%20County.md) and 7 more, at $1,798 a month, and cheapest in [Benton County](./Tennessee/Benton%20County.md), [Campbell County](./Tennessee/Campbell%20County.md), [Carroll County](./Tennessee/Carroll%20County.md) and 37 more, at $995. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Tennessee does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 29% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Tennessee's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Chester County](./Tennessee/Chester%20County.md) and [Madison County](./Tennessee/Madison%20County.md) (38%), least in [Moore County](./Tennessee/Moore%20County.md) (24.3%).

**Has rent outrun income in Tennessee?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 90 of Tennessee's 95 counties with both, and slower in 5.
