## Questions

**How has rent moved in Pennsylvania?** The median two-bedroom rent of Pennsylvania's 67 counties rose from $413 in FY1995 to $1,136 in FY2027, 175.1%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Pennsylvania is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Bucks County](./Pennsylvania/Bucks%20County.md), [Chester County](./Pennsylvania/Chester%20County.md), [Delaware County](./Pennsylvania/Delaware%20County.md), [Montgomery County](./Pennsylvania/Montgomery%20County.md) and [Philadelphia County](./Pennsylvania/Philadelphia%20County.md), at $1,860 a month, and cheapest in [Bedford County](./Pennsylvania/Bedford%20County.md), [Cambria County](./Pennsylvania/Cambria%20County.md), [Cameron County](./Pennsylvania/Cameron%20County.md) and 19 more, at $1,014. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Pennsylvania does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 28.7% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Pennsylvania's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Carbon County](./Pennsylvania/Carbon%20County.md), [Lehigh County](./Pennsylvania/Lehigh%20County.md) and [Northampton County](./Pennsylvania/Northampton%20County.md) (37.6%), least in [Union County](./Pennsylvania/Union%20County.md) (23.3%).

**Has rent outrun income in Pennsylvania?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 59 of Pennsylvania's 67 counties with both, and slower in 8.
