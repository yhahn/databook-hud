## Questions

**How has rent moved in Kentucky?** The median two-bedroom rent of Kentucky's 120 counties rose from $319 in FY1995 to $925 in FY2027, 190.4%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Kentucky is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Boone County](./Kentucky/Boone%20County.md), [Bracken County](./Kentucky/Bracken%20County.md), [Campbell County](./Kentucky/Campbell%20County.md), [Gallatin County](./Kentucky/Gallatin%20County.md), [Kenton County](./Kentucky/Kenton%20County.md) and [Pendleton County](./Kentucky/Pendleton%20County.md), at $1,442 a month, and cheapest in [Adair County](./Kentucky/Adair%20County.md), [Bath County](./Kentucky/Bath%20County.md), [Bell County](./Kentucky/Bell%20County.md) and 52 more, at $909. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Kentucky does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 28.9% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Kentucky's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Christian County](./Kentucky/Christian%20County.md) and [Trigg County](./Kentucky/Trigg%20County.md) (34.4%), least in [Lyon County](./Kentucky/Lyon%20County.md) (22.7%).

**Has rent outrun income in Kentucky?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 97 of Kentucky's 120 counties with both, and slower in 23.
