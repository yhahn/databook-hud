## Questions

**How has rent moved in Connecticut?** The median two-bedroom rent of Connecticut's 169 towns rose from $697 in FY1995 to $1,933 in FY2027, 177.3%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Connecticut is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Bridgeport town](./Connecticut/Greater%20Bridgeport%20Planning%20Region/Bridgeport%20town.md), [Easton town](./Connecticut/Greater%20Bridgeport%20Planning%20Region/Easton%20town.md), [Fairfield town](./Connecticut/Greater%20Bridgeport%20Planning%20Region/Fairfield%20town.md) and 21 more, at $2,399 a month, and cheapest in [Barkhamsted town](./Connecticut/Northwest%20Hills%20Planning%20Region/Barkhamsted%20town.md), [Burlington town](./Connecticut/Northwest%20Hills%20Planning%20Region/Burlington%20town.md), [Canaan town](./Connecticut/Northwest%20Hills%20Planning%20Region/Canaan%20town.md) and 18 more, at $1,560. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Connecticut does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 34.6% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Connecticut's towns (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Bridgewater town](./Connecticut/Western%20Connecticut%20Planning%20Region/Bridgewater%20town.md) and [New Milford town](./Connecticut/Western%20Connecticut%20Planning%20Region/New%20Milford%20town.md) (44%), least in [Union town](./Connecticut/Northeastern%20Connecticut%20Planning%20Region/Union%20town.md) (30.4%).

**Has rent outrun income in Connecticut?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 169 of Connecticut's 169 towns with both, and slower in 0.
