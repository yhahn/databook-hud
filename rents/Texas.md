## Questions

**How has rent moved in Texas?** The median two-bedroom rent of Texas's 254 counties rose from $344 in FY1995 to $1,105 in FY2027, 221.1%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Texas is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Bastrop County](./Texas/Bastrop%20County.md), [Caldwell County](./Texas/Caldwell%20County.md), [Hays County](./Texas/Hays%20County.md), [Travis County](./Texas/Travis%20County.md) and [Williamson County](./Texas/Williamson%20County.md), at $1,817 a month, and cheapest in [Baylor County](./Texas/Baylor%20County.md), [Bosque County](./Texas/Bosque%20County.md), [Brooks County](./Texas/Brooks%20County.md) and 59 more, at $1,014. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Texas does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 29.1% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Texas's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Goliad County](./Texas/Goliad%20County.md) and [Victoria County](./Texas/Victoria%20County.md) (41.2%), least in [Glasscock County](./Texas/Glasscock%20County.md) (20%).

**Has rent outrun income in Texas?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 203 of Texas's 254 counties with both, and slower in 51.
