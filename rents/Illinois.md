## Questions

**How has rent moved in Illinois?** The median two-bedroom rent of Illinois's 102 counties rose from $337 in FY1995 to $958 in FY2027, 184.5%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Illinois is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Kendall County](./Illinois/Kendall%20County.md), at $2,341 a month, and cheapest in [Carroll County](./Illinois/Carroll%20County.md), [Cass County](./Illinois/Cass%20County.md), [Clay County](./Illinois/Clay%20County.md) and 38 more, at $930. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Illinois does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 24.5% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Illinois's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Kendall County](./Illinois/Kendall%20County.md) (36.2%), least in [Effingham County](./Illinois/Effingham%20County.md) (21%).

**Has rent outrun income in Illinois?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 89 of Illinois's 102 counties with both, and slower in 13.
