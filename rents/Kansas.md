## Questions

**How has rent moved in Kansas?** The median two-bedroom rent of Kansas's 105 counties rose from $319 in FY1995 to $961 in FY2027, 201.3%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Kansas is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Johnson County](./Kansas/Johnson%20County.md), [Leavenworth County](./Kansas/Leavenworth%20County.md), [Linn County](./Kansas/Linn%20County.md), [Miami County](./Kansas/Miami%20County.md) and [Wyandotte County](./Kansas/Wyandotte%20County.md), at $1,546 a month, and cheapest in [Allen County](./Kansas/Allen%20County.md), [Anderson County](./Kansas/Anderson%20County.md), [Atchison County](./Kansas/Atchison%20County.md) and 61 more, at $961. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Kansas does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 24.6% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Kansas's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Kearny County](./Kansas/Kearny%20County.md) (37%), least in [Thomas County](./Kansas/Thomas%20County.md) (20.3%).

**Has rent outrun income in Kansas?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 61 of Kansas's 105 counties with both, and slower in 44.
