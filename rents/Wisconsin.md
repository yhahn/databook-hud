## Questions

**How has rent moved in Wisconsin?** The median two-bedroom rent of Wisconsin's 72 counties rose from $367 in FY1995 to $1,041 in FY2027, 183.5%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Wisconsin is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Pierce County](./Wisconsin/Pierce%20County.md) and [St. Croix County](./Wisconsin/St.%20Croix%20County.md), at $1,734 a month, and cheapest in [Adams County](./Wisconsin/Adams%20County.md), [Ashland County](./Wisconsin/Ashland%20County.md), [Bayfield County](./Wisconsin/Bayfield%20County.md) and 26 more, at $1,014. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Wisconsin does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 24.4% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Wisconsin's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Pierce County](./Wisconsin/Pierce%20County.md) and [St. Croix County](./Wisconsin/St.%20Croix%20County.md) (31.2%), least in [Portage County](./Wisconsin/Portage%20County.md) (23.4%).

**Has rent outrun income in Wisconsin?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 67 of Wisconsin's 72 counties with both, and slower in 5.
