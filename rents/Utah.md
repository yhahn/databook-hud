HUD's Fair Market Rents for Utah, fiscal years 1983 to 2027: 29 counties, each with its rents by bedroom count and the FMR areas it has sat in. 3 of them have changed FMR area at least once (not counting 2006, when HUD renumbered every area). Its income limits are on its [limits page](../limits/Utah.md), its tax credit housing on its [tax credits page](../lihtc/Utah.md).

## Questions

**How has rent moved in Utah?** The median two-bedroom rent of Utah's 29 counties rose from $374 in FY1995 to $1,158 in FY2027, 209.6%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Utah is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Summit County](./Utah/Summit%20County.md), at $2,153 a month, and cheapest in [Carbon County](./Utah/Carbon%20County.md), [Emery County](./Utah/Emery%20County.md), [Garfield County](./Utah/Garfield%20County.md) and [Uintah County](./Utah/Uintah%20County.md), at $1,014. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Utah does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 26% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Utah's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Kane County](./Utah/Kane%20County.md) (37.5%), least in [Emery County](./Utah/Emery%20County.md), [Rich County](./Utah/Rich%20County.md) and [Wayne County](./Utah/Wayne%20County.md) (22%).

**Has rent outrun income in Utah?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 16 of Utah's 29 counties with both, and slower in 13.
