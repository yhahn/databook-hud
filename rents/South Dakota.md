## Questions

**How has rent moved in South Dakota?** The median two-bedroom rent of South Dakota's 66 counties rose from $346 in FY1995 to $1,033 in FY2027, 198.6%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in South Dakota is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Pennington County](./South%20Dakota/Pennington%20County.md), at $1,430 a month, and cheapest in [Aurora County](./South%20Dakota/Aurora%20County.md), [Bon Homme County](./South%20Dakota/Bon%20Homme%20County.md), [Brown County](./South%20Dakota/Brown%20County.md) and 26 more, at $1,014. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in South Dakota does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 23.4% of the very low income limit for four in the median of South Dakota's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Pennington County](./South%20Dakota/Pennington%20County.md) (31.7%), least in [Marshall County](./South%20Dakota/Marshall%20County.md) (19.9%).

**Has rent outrun income in South Dakota?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 20 of South Dakota's 66 counties with both, and slower in 46.
