## Questions

**How has rent moved in Maine?** The median two-bedroom rent of Maine's 529 towns rose from $435 in FY1995 to $1,367 in FY2027, 214.3%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Maine is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Berwick town](./Maine/York%20County/Berwick%20town.md), [Eliot town](./Maine/York%20County/Eliot%20town.md), [Kittery town](./Maine/York%20County/Kittery%20town.md), [South Berwick town](./Maine/York%20County/South%20Berwick%20town.md) and [York town](./Maine/York%20County/York%20town.md), at $2,376 a month, and cheapest in [Allagash town](./Maine/Aroostook%20County/Allagash%20town.md), [Amity town](./Maine/Aroostook%20County/Amity%20town.md), [Ashland town](./Maine/Aroostook%20County/Ashland%20town.md) and 68 more, at $1,175. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Maine does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 35.4% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Maine's towns (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Auburn city](./Maine/Androscoggin%20County/Auburn%20city.md), [Durham town](./Maine/Androscoggin%20County/Durham%20town.md), [Greene town](./Maine/Androscoggin%20County/Greene%20town.md) and 11 more (40.5%), least in [Allagash town](./Maine/Aroostook%20County/Allagash%20town.md), [Amity town](./Maine/Aroostook%20County/Amity%20town.md), [Ashland town](./Maine/Aroostook%20County/Ashland%20town.md) and 68 more (32%).

**Has rent outrun income in Maine?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 523 of Maine's 523 towns with both, and slower in 0.
