## Questions

**How has rent moved in North Dakota?** The median two-bedroom rent of North Dakota's 53 counties rose from $291 in FY1995 to $1,014 in FY2027, 248.5%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in North Dakota is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Divide County](./North%20Dakota/Divide%20County.md), at $1,858 a month, and cheapest in [Adams County](./North%20Dakota/Adams%20County.md), [Benson County](./North%20Dakota/Benson%20County.md), [Billings County](./North%20Dakota/Billings%20County.md) and 28 more, at $1,014. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in North Dakota does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 19.6% of the very low income limit for four in the median of North Dakota's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Divide County](./North%20Dakota/Divide%20County.md) (30.3%), least in [Foster County](./North%20Dakota/Foster%20County.md) (17.2%).

**Has rent outrun income in North Dakota?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 19 of North Dakota's 53 counties with both, and slower in 34.
