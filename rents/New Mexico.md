## Questions

**How has rent moved in New Mexico?** The median two-bedroom rent of New Mexico's 33 counties rose from $353 in FY1995 to $1,063 in FY2027, 201.1%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in New Mexico is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Los Alamos County](./New%20Mexico/Los%20Alamos%20County.md), at $1,874 a month, and cheapest in [Colfax County](./New%20Mexico/Colfax%20County.md), [De Baca County](./New%20Mexico/De%20Baca%20County.md), [Guadalupe County](./New%20Mexico/Guadalupe%20County.md) and 9 more, at $1,014. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in New Mexico does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 32.4% of the very low income limit for four in the median of New Mexico's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Taos County](./New%20Mexico/Taos%20County.md) (38.1%), least in [Los Alamos County](./New%20Mexico/Los%20Alamos%20County.md) (24.4%).

**Has rent outrun income in New Mexico?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 28 of New Mexico's 33 counties with both, and slower in 5.
