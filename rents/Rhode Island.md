## Questions

**How has rent moved in Rhode Island?** The median two-bedroom rent of Rhode Island's 39 towns rose from $638 in FY1995 to $1,879 in FY2027, 194.5%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Rhode Island is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Middletown town](./Rhode%20Island/Newport%20County/Middletown%20town.md), [Newport city](./Rhode%20Island/Newport%20County/Newport%20city.md) and [Portsmouth town](./Rhode%20Island/Newport%20County/Portsmouth%20town.md), at $2,507 a month, and cheapest in [Hopkinton town](./Rhode%20Island/Washington%20County/Hopkinton%20town.md), [New Shoreham town](./Rhode%20Island/Washington%20County/New%20Shoreham%20town.md) and [Westerly town](./Rhode%20Island/Washington%20County/Westerly%20town.md), at $1,832. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Rhode Island does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 37% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Rhode Island's towns (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Middletown town](./Rhode%20Island/Newport%20County/Middletown%20town.md), [Newport city](./Rhode%20Island/Newport%20County/Newport%20city.md) and [Portsmouth town](./Rhode%20Island/Newport%20County/Portsmouth%20town.md) (40.3%), least in [Hopkinton town](./Rhode%20Island/Washington%20County/Hopkinton%20town.md), [New Shoreham town](./Rhode%20Island/Washington%20County/New%20Shoreham%20town.md) and [Westerly town](./Rhode%20Island/Washington%20County/Westerly%20town.md) (32%).

**Has rent outrun income in Rhode Island?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 39 of Rhode Island's 39 towns with both, and slower in 0.
