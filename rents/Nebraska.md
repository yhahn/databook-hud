## Questions

**How has rent moved in Nebraska?** The median two-bedroom rent of Nebraska's 93 counties rose from $337 in FY1995 to $1,014 in FY2027, 200.9%; the nation's median county's rose 190.9% in the same years.

**Where in Nebraska is rent dearest, and where cheapest?** In FY2027 a two-bedroom home rents dearest in [Cass County](./Nebraska/Cass%20County.md), [Douglas County](./Nebraska/Douglas%20County.md), [Sarpy County](./Nebraska/Sarpy%20County.md) and [Washington County](./Nebraska/Washington%20County.md), at $1,450 a month, and cheapest in [Adams County](./Nebraska/Adams%20County.md), [Antelope County](./Nebraska/Antelope%20County.md), [Arthur County](./Nebraska/Arthur%20County.md) and 63 more, at $1,014. Every place is a row of the grid below, dearest first, its rent every fourth year since FY1995.

**Where in Nebraska does rent take most of a low income?** Twelve months of the FY2026 two-bedroom rent are 24.5% of the very low income limit for four in the median of Nebraska's counties (28.8% in the nation's median county); most in [Hall County](./Nebraska/Hall%20County.md) (31%), least in [Hamilton County](./Nebraska/Hamilton%20County.md) (20.3%).

**Has rent outrun income in Nebraska?** From FY2006 to FY2026 the two-bedroom rent rose faster than the median family income in 55 of Nebraska's 93 counties with both, and slower in 38.
